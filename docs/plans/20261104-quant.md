Use fixture transcripts to guide the choice of quantizer for a model's tensors.

      Q4_K stores 256 weights in 144 file bytes with
      `weight = code · d · scale - dmin · min`, keeps 128 bytes of codes on the GPU, and lets a
      row of 288 share a block with the next row; the GGUF reader rejects counts that are not
      multiples of 256, Parakeet's CPU decoder expands Q4_K to f32, and Q4_K convolution rows
      needing extra columns expand to padded F32.

Moonshine Tiny's published Q8_0 file, re-quantized round-to-nearest to Q5_0: 97 tensors and
25,362,432 weights, each row a multiple of 32. The conv stem is already F32 in that file.
The file is 35,466,912 bytes as Q8_0 and 25,956,000 bytes as Q5_0.

On an RX 5700 XT the loader stacks some of those tensors and uploads 67 linears. The weight
and scale buffers hold every converted weight in 21,235,968 bytes: codes packed at 24 bytes
per 32 weights, plus the f16 scale, rows padded to a multiple of 64. The same padded matrices
as f16 would be 52,273,152 bytes.

`jfk` matches the Q8_0 transcript. In `dots`, Q8_0 has "something, your gut" where Q5_0 has
"something. You're gut". `jobs-silence` is "I'm going to put the other one in the middle of the
room." as Q8_0 and "I'm going to be able to practice it." as Q5_0.

For Q4_K_M, the same file is re-quantized round-to-nearest: 33 tensors stay Q5_0 and the other
64 become Q4_K. The Q5_0 tensors are the token embedding, `dec.blocks.0.ffn.fc1`, every
`cross_attn.out`, and the attention and `ffn.fc1` of `enc.blocks.0` through `enc.blocks.4`.
q, k and v of one attention are one upload and share a type. The file is 24,525,216 bytes,
and `general.file_type` is 15.

On the same GPU the loader uploads 67 linears, 44 of them Q4_K and 23 Q5_0. The weight and
scale buffers are 18,406,368 bytes, against 52,273,152 for the same padded matrices as f16.

Word errors are whitespace-separated tokens against the Q8_0 fixture text. Punctuation stays
on its token.

`jfk` and `dots` match the Q8_0 text. `jobs-silence` is "I'm going to put it on the top of".

The token embedding at Q4_K turns the second "ask" in `jfk` into "as". Encoder layers 0 and 3
at Q4_K do that too, or add a comma after "Americans". Encoder layers 1, 2 and 4 can each be
Q4_K alone and stay inside the Q5_0 error, and the three together turn that "ask" into "as",
so they stay Q5_0. Dropping every decoder `ffn.fc1` raises `jobs-silence` to 15/13.
`dec.blocks.0.ffn.fc1` is the decoder `fc1` these clips still need at Q5_0. Putting every
`cross_attn.out` at Q4_K, with its k and v still Q5_0, raises `jobs-silence` the same way.
