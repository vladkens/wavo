Moonshine Tiny's published Q8_0 file, re-quantized round-to-nearest to Q5_0: 97 tensors and
25,362,432 weights, each row a multiple of 32. The conv stem is already F32 in that file.
The file is 35,466,912 bytes as Q8_0 and 25,956,000 bytes as Q5_0.

On an RX 5700 XT the loader stacks some of those tensors and uploads 67 linears. The weight
and scale buffers hold every converted weight in 21,235,968 bytes: codes packed at 24 bytes
per 32 weights, plus the f16 scale, rows padded to a multiple of 64. The same padded matrices
as f16 would be 52,273,152 bytes.

"something. You're gut". `jobs-silence` is "I'm going to put the other one in the middle of the
room." as Q8_0 and "I'm going to be able to practice it." as Q5_0.
